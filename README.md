# Ethan Brown

M.A. Economics student at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, with dual B.S. degrees in Economics and Data Analytics from Berry College. I work on applied microeconomics and causal inference, with particular interest in environmental and energy economics, the economics of crime, and public policy evaluation.

## Selected projects

- [Short term rental regulation and home values](https://github.com/ethanbrownecon/sandpoint-str-regulation-housing-values): synthetic difference in differences on Sandpoint, Idaho's 2018 ordinance.
- [Marijuana decriminalization and crime](https://github.com/ethanbrownecon/marijuana-decriminalization-did): difference in differences on Atlanta's 2017 policy.
- [Airbnb price prediction](https://github.com/ethanbrownecon/Final-Project-Airbnb-Price-Prediction-Geospatial-Analysis): six machine learning models on more than 100,000 listings.
- [GPS horse race simulator](https://github.com/ethanbrownecon/gps-horse-race-simulator): predictive modeling and an interactive dashboard built for The Econ Games 2026.
- [Technical skills for economics](https://github.com/ethanbrownecon/econ-toolkit): an open guide to Git, R, Stata, Python, SQL, and reproducible research.
